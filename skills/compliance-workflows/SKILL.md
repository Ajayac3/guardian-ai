# Skill: Compliance workflows
Shipping checklist for store review.

## Play Console (FGS)
- [ ] Declare each FGS type (microphone, camera, location) with use case
      "Emergency SOS / live video call to emergency contact".
- [ ] Record the demo video showing owner-initiated activation + recording notice.
- [ ] Data Safety form; privacy policy URL; account/data deletion.

## App Store
- [ ] Visible recording indicator; Privacy Manifest; nutrition labels.
- [ ] Justify background modes; no covert recording; account deletion.
